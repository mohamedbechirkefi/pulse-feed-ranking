## S4. Robustness results

1. **Within-experiment factors (confirmatory).** Feedback noise and initial concentration (EXP-001), drift rate and initial concentration (EXP-002), catalog skew and choice sharpness (EXP-003): H1 supported in every cell (manuscript Tables 2, 4, 5).
2. **Bootstrap check (confirmatory).** For every primary contrast the percentile bootstrap interval agreed with the t-interval in sign and in the frozen decision (reports, section 8).
3. **Second test on real-data-derived inputs (confirmatory).** EXP-004 (manuscript Table 6).
4. **Drift form (confirmatory, weak).** Additive-then-clip check (manuscript Table 4; limitations 12).
5. **Like rate (exploratory, M4).** Manuscript section 5.5 and Table 7; full table with observed like rates in `results/exploratory/m4-likerate-summary.csv`; figure `research/paper/figures/FIG-M4-X01.svg`. Fitted midpoints (frozen method of protocol 15.5, calibration seeds 700001 to 700020): 0.406769 (target 0.05), 0.281712 (0.15), 0.188523 (0.30), 0.120266 (0.45), 0.066421 (0.577, frozen), 0.010428 (0.70), -0.081000 (0.85), and the planning value 0.5 (0.0206) (`results/exploratory/m4-likerate-fit.json`).
6. **Independent recomputation (M4 audit).** Every contrast, summary row, Welch interval and drift-form value was recomputed from the raw run files without the project's statistics code; no mismatch (`research/publication/evidence_audit.md`).
